Mail dashboard

One place for my own inboxes

Mail dashboard is a personal tool built by Prajjwal Lekhi for his own use. It shows his Gmail inboxes on one screen, sorts each thread by how much it needs his attention, and lets him reply without opening Gmail.

It runs only on his own computer. It is not a product, it has no sign-up, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Read how it handles email data in the privacy policy.
